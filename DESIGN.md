# OppScout AI design notes

**Subject:** a guide that helps students and graduates in Nigeria find opportunities and judge their fit, and helps organizations list them. The job of every screen is to answer "does this fit me, and when does it close?"

**Palette** (cool paper, not cream, so it does not read as a template):
- Paper `#F4F6FB`, Surface `#FFFFFF`, Ink `#12172F`, Body `#3A4260`, Muted `#667089`, Line `#E1E5F0`
- Cobalt `#2447F5` (actions, fit), Saffron `#F4A21B` (deadlines, urgency), Green `#15805A` (met), Red `#C7362B` (not met), Deep `#0F1535` (organization band)

**Type:** Bricolage Grotesque for headings and numbers (characterful, confident), Public Sans for text (built for plain readability in forms and requirements). Both self-hosted.

**One memorable thing:** the *fit ring*, a dial that shows how well a listing matches a profile, paired with a deadline chip that turns saffron as a closing date nears. Everything else stays quiet: borders over shadows, one accent, no decoration.

**Rules we follow:** no all-caps labels, no dot-joined metadata strings, no numbered markers unless the content is a sequence, no one-word headline accents. Radii differ by role (cards 16, controls 10, chips full). Motion is limited to the rings drawing in on the home page, and it respects reduced-motion settings.

**Honesty in the interface:** a fit score is relevance, not eligibility, and the page says so. Listings say who posted them and whether the organization is verified. The home page's sample matches are computed live from a stated sample profile.
