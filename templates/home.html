How it works

1. Tell us about you

Add your country, education, skills and interests. Every field is optional, and your profile stays in your browser.

2. See what fits

Listings are filtered by who can apply, then ranked by how well they match, with the reasons shown. A score is relevance, not an eligibility decision.

3. Check the requirements

Each listing shows its published requirements and a link to the official page. AI eligibility and skill-gap checks are coming next.
