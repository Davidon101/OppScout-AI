Post your opportunities on OppScout AI

Employers, universities, scholarship providers and NGOs can list jobs, internships, scholarships, grants, fellowships and training here. Students and graduates find them in the public directory and through AI matching.

How this works, and its limits.
  • There are no accounts or passwords. When you register you get a management key. Keep it safe: anyone who has it can manage your listings, and we cannot recover it for you.
  • Every new or edited listing is reviewed by an OppScout administrator before it goes live.
  • Once live, you can take it offline, close applications, mark it filled or archive it yourself.
  • This is an early prototype. Do not post anything confidential.

What you will provide

  • Your organization's name, type, official email, website and location.
  • For each opportunity: title, category, description, who can apply, requirements, deadline, official application link, and a few fields specific to the category (for example salary range for jobs, or what the funding covers for scholarships).
